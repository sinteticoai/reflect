# Challenge 1: Predict which survivors will need acute care

Track: Healthcare (set by Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center), area: Survivorship
Source: https://hackathon.miamiaihub.com/challenges (pulled 2026-10-17)
Box folder: https://miami.box.com/s/l1fkmqz1h7rzsx78irsr49laf4tkupoy

You have 2017 survey answers from real survivors, their 2018 health care use for a development set, and a separate set of synthetic clinical records for different people. Predict which survivors will need acute care in 2018, identify the factors associated with that risk, and show whether the clinical records add anything beyond what the survey alone can tell us.
