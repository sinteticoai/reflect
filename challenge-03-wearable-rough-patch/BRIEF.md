# Challenge 3: Warn before a rough patch, from wearable data

Track: Healthcare (set by Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center), area: Lifestyle medicine
Source: https://hackathon.miamiaihub.com/challenges (pulled 2026-10-17)
Box folder: https://miami.box.com/s/p3vdbg5bpzdehxguu99koxgxm8zrf0f5

People going through cancer treatment often wear a fitness tracker. Some weeks are rough: less energy, worse sleep, fewer steps. Coaches usually hear about it days later, at the next call. Build an app that watches someone's wearable data and warns when a rough patch is coming. For the patient, their coach, or both.
