# Challenge 4: Show how meals, meal timing and movement shape blood sugar

Track: Healthcare (set by Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center), area: Lifestyle medicine
Source: https://hackathon.miamiaihub.com/challenges (pulled 2026-10-17)
Box folder: https://miami.box.com/s/tis2ig38pnkkd7t6v1nzsks8t5cvg4em

People who finish cancer treatment ask fair questions. Does a late dinner matter? Is an evening walk worth it? How much movement is enough? Programs that coach healthy eating, overnight fasting and exercise need clear answers. Build a tool or app that shows people how their meals, meal times and movement shape their blood sugar and health, and turns that into advice they can follow.
