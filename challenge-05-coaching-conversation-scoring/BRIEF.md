# Challenge 5: Follow and score a health-coaching conversation

Track: Healthcare (set by Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center), area: Lifestyle medicine
Source: https://hackathon.miamiaihub.com/challenges (pulled 2026-10-17)
Box folder: https://miami.box.com/s/q7fd8k1dm5dn6djw2v70ki3jxf4wauun

Health coaching programs for people with cancer run on conversations. Good coaches use motivational interviewing: open questions and listening back, not lecturing. Programs record thousands of calls and can review only a handful. Build an AI app that follows a coaching conversation, live or recorded, spoken or texted: tag what's happening, score the coach, and flag anything a person should see.
