# Challenge 6: From a compound to its targets, cell lines and cancer types

Track: Healthcare (set by Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center), area: Precision medicine & drug discovery
Source: https://hackathon.miamiaihub.com/challenges (pulled 2026-10-17)
Box folder: https://miami.box.com/s/8ru4ny1seo75wrkfsopajzk6kn9rrrcb

When a new compound shows promise against cancer, two questions come up almost immediately. What does it bind to? And which cancers does it actually work against? You start with a compound, given as a SMILES string. It might be one from the dataset or one you have never seen before. Work out which proteins it is likely to bind, which cancer cell lines are likely to respond to it, what cancer types it could be useful for, and a plausible explanation of why.
