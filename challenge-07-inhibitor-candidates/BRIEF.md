# Challenge 7: Propose and rank new inhibitor candidates

Track: Healthcare (set by Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center), area: Precision medicine & drug discovery
Source: https://hackathon.miamiaihub.com/challenges (pulled 2026-10-17)
Box folder: https://miami.box.com/s/8ru4ny1seo75wrkfsopajzk6kn9rrrcb

Starting with a protein target, a cancer cell line, or both, propose new compounds that might inhibit that target or slow the growth of that cancer cell. Rank your candidate compounds, taking into account predicted target activity, predicted sensitivity of the cell line, selectivity, and drug-likeness. You will need your own compound generation approach, your own models to score what you generate, and a way to check that the molecules are chemically valid, drug-like and plausible enough to trust.
